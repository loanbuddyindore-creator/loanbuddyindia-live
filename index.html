Transparent Rate & Fee Information

Every charge below is disclosed upfront. Where a value depends on the lender you're finally matched with, we say so clearly instead of quoting a number we can't guarantee.

Interest Rate

See the current indicative starting rate for each loan type in the Loan Products and Compare Loans sections above.

Varies by lender & profile

Processing Fee

A one-time fee charged by the bank/NBFC for processing your application, typically deducted from the disbursed amount.

Varies by lender — disclosed before you accept any offer

Documentation Charges

Covers legal/technical verification of documents. Some lenders bundle this into the processing fee; others itemise it separately.

Varies by lender

Government Charges

Statutory charges such as stamp duty, GST on fees, and franking — set by government, not by us or the lender.

Fixed by government / statute

Foreclosure Charges

Applicable if you close your loan fully before the tenure ends. Many floating-rate retail loans carry nil foreclosure charges as per RBI/NHB guidelines — always confirmed in your sanction letter.

Varies by lender & loan type

Part-Payment Charges

Applicable if you pay a portion of your outstanding principal ahead of schedule, reducing tenure or EMI.

Varies by lender

Balance Transfer Charges

Covers moving your existing loan to a new lender for a lower rate — includes a switch fee at the new lender and possible foreclosure fee at the old one.

Varies by lender

Taxes

GST is applicable on processing fees and most service charges, as per prevailing government rates.

As per prevailing GST rate
